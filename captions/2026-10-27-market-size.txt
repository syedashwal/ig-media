How big are the markets you trade?

Forex: about $9.6 trillion traded every day (BIS survey, April 2025).
Gold (XAUUSD): about $227 billion traded every day (World Gold Council, 2024).
Indian equities: about $5 trillion in total listed value (mid-2026).
Crypto: about $2 trillion in total value (mid-2026).

Different yardsticks, approximate figures. The point is the same: size gives you liquidity, not an edge. Risk decides if you stay in the game.
