Does price fill the whole gap? Not always.

1. Full fill. Price returns and closes the entire gap. Common in slower markets.
2. Half fill (50%). Strong markets often only reach the middle of the gap before moving on.
3. No fill. Very strong moves can leave the gap behind for a long time.

Gap = context.

The gap is a zone, not a promise. Save this and send it to a trader who needs it.
