Today is FOMC.

The Federal Open Market Committee is the group inside the US Federal Reserve that sets interest rates. 12 members vote, and they meet 8 times a year.

Hawkish news (higher rate expectations) tends to lift the dollar and push gold down. Dovish news (lower rate expectations) tends to do the opposite. Tends to, not always: price often whips both ways in seconds around the release.

Decide your risk before the release, not during it.
