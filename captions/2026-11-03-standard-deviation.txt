How far can a move travel? Standard Deviation projections give you the map.

Take one swing and measure it from end to end. Then project that size out beyond it. Each level is one to watch, not a target. Price can stop at any level, or at none.

Level dekhna hai, andha trade nahi lena.

The chart is an illustration, not a trade.
