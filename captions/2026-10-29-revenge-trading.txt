You took a loss. The loss is over. The urge to get it back is not.

Revenge trading is not a strategy. It's stress asking for the money back: heart rate up, size up, rules gone.

The fix is boring and it works. Close the chart. Walk away for 15 minutes. Come back and take only the setup your rules would have given you anyway.
