Is greed trading for you? 3 signs.

1. You move your target mid-trade.
2. You add to a winner with no plan.
3. You call a profit "too small".

Target likha tha? Wahi exit hai.

Write your exit before you enter. When price gets there, take it. The trade you planned is the only one that's yours.
