BSL and SSL, explained in 30 seconds.

BSL = Buy-Side Liquidity: orders resting above highs. Sellers' stops sit there, and breakout traders' buy orders.
SSL = Sell-Side Liquidity: orders resting below lows. Buyers' stops sit there, and breakout traders' sell orders.

ICT's view is that price often reaches for these pools to fill bigger orders. Spotting them gives you context. It is not a signal, and it is not a reason to enter.
