Why losing is easy and recovering is hard: drawdown math.

1. Down 10%? Need +11%. $10,000 to $9,000 needs $1,000 back, which is 11% of what's left.
2. Down 20%? Need +25%. $8,000 has to earn $2,000 just to get back to even.
3. Down 50%? Need +100%. $5,000 has to double. That's why big losses end careers.
