FVG vs Order Block. Both are zones. Fark kya hai?

Order Block: the last opposite candle before a big move. It shows where orders were sitting.
FVG: three candles, and the gap between the wicks of candle 1 and candle 3. It shows where price left in a hurry.

Both are context, not entries.
