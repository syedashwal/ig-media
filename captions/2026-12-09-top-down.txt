One chart is never enough. Top down.

1. Higher timeframe: direction. The daily and 4H tell you which side the market favours.
2. Middle timeframe: the zone. The 1H shows where price is likely to react.
3. Lower timeframe: the reaction. The 15m shows whether price respects the zone. Context, not the entry model.
