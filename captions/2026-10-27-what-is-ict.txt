What is ICT, in 60 seconds?

ICT stands for Inner Circle Trader, a way of reading price taught by Michael Huddleston. The core idea: price isn't random, it moves toward liquidity. Stops sit above highs and below lows, structure shows direction, gaps show what price may come back to, and time of day decides when it moves.

It's a map, not a button. Knowing it doesn't pay you. Risk decides if you stay in the game.
