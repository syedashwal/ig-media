Gold doesn't move all day. Know the hours.

Asia: often a range.
London open: about 1:30 PM IST (08:00 UTC).
New York: about 6:30 to 9:30 PM IST (13:00 to 16:00 UTC).

Sahi time, sahi trade.

Trade the hours when the volume shows up, not the whole day.
