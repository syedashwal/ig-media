Stop loss hit hua… and then price ran your way?

That's not luck. That's liquidity. In an uptrend, everyone's stops sit under the higher lows line. Price goes there, takes the stops, then moves. This is called a liquidity sweep.

Concept, not a signal.

Next time, before placing your stop, socho: is this everyone's stop?
