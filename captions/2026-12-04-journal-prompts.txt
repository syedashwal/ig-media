Journal prompts that actually work.

1. What did I feel before entry? Calm, bored, angry, rushed? The feeling predicts the trade.
2. Was this in my plan? Yes or no. No explanations.
3. What did I do after a loss? This is where accounts are saved or blown.
4. What would I tell a friend? You're kinder and smarter about other people's trades.
5. One thing to repeat. Not only mistakes. Write down what went right.
