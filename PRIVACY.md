# Privacy policy: Ashwal XAU Publisher

Ashwal XAU Publisher is a private tool used only by Ashwal (@ashwal.xau) to upload his own short videos to his own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

- It uses Google sign-in only to upload videos to the owner's channel (YouTube Data API, `youtube.upload` and `youtube.readonly` scopes).
- It does not collect, sell or share any personal data from anyone else.
- Access tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets in this repository and are used only by its own scheduled workflows.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
